# Product Philosophy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Stable  
**Review:** Every 6 months

## Why we exist

We optimize for uninterrupted thinking.

Every feature must reduce cognitive effort.

Users should feel faster before they actually become faster.

We explain, never overwhelm.

The editor should feel alive, never noisy.

Every click should increase confidence.

Every interaction should preserve flow.

The editor is a partner, not another tool.

## Win condition

> I always know what is happening. I always know what to do next. The editor never gets in my way. When something breaks, it helps me understand and recover.

## Engineering allocation

- 15% platform
- 25% performance / reliability
- 60% product experience
